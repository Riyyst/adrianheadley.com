Projects

A couple of current projects and ideas I’m working on. The platform continues to evolve, with new tools and improvements being added to enhance everyday usability.

Tools

Tools is a collection of practical browser tools I wanted in one place. The aim is simple: make common jobs quick, keep the interface clean, and process as much as possible on your own device.

  • Curated links to practical tools and resources.
  • Designed as a simple “launchpad” for day-to-day tasks.
  • Built to evolve over time as new tools are added.

Orthodox Calendar and Study Bible

I brought the Greek and Russian Orthodox calendars together with an Orthodox Study Bible reader so the daily calendar, scripture and study material can all be reached from one place.

  • Includes both Greek and Russian calendar views with daily commemorations, fasting guidance, and feast days.
  • Adds an Orthodox Study Bible section so scripture can be read alongside the calendar.
  • Keeps the experience simple, reflective, and easy to use across desktop and mobile.

ChurchFinder

ChurchFinder is a longer-term project I am building to make churches across the UK easier to find and understand, whether you are looking locally or visiting somewhere new.

  • Focus on clear information: location, denomination, and key details.
  • Built with the idea of mapping and search at its core.
  • Early-stage work in progress, with plenty of room to grow.
